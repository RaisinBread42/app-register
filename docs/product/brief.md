# Product brief — App Register

**Company:** Obsidian Software  
**Working name:** App Register  
**Date:** 2026-11-21  
**Evidence:** [docs/research/saas-app-register-validation-2026-11-21.md](../research/saas-app-register-validation-2026-11-21.md)

## Problem

Lean IT teams (1–5 people) at companies of about 30–500 staff track bought SaaS and in-house apps in spreadsheets that go stale. The renewal date is not the real deadline — the **notice deadline** often sits 60–90 days earlier — and ownership walks out when someone leaves. Enterprise SaaS-management platforms are too expensive and slow to set up for this segment; spend tools ignore in-house apps.

## Users

- Primary: IT manager or sole admin on a 1–5 person IT team supporting 30–500 staff.
- Secondary: a backup owner named on each app; later, MSP operators without a PSA (out of MVP focus).

## Positioning (spec v1.0, 2026-11-28)

> **Know what your applications depend on — and what needs attention.**
>
> Monitor app health, dependencies, software support, vendor notices, hosting changes, and renewal deadlines in one calm workspace.

App Register is an application health and lifecycle monitoring service for lean IT teams (1–5 people) in organisations of about 30–500 staff. It tracks bought SaaS and internally built apps, ties relevant changes to the apps they affect, and surfaces the next useful action ([spec v1.0](spec-v1.md) §1.1).

## What we sell

One register for **apps you buy** and **apps your people build**: every record has a named owner, a backup owner, a renewal date, a separate notice deadline, and (for in-house apps) where it runs, what data and keys it touches, and what breaks if it stops.

## What we do not sell (now)

Automatic discovery, usage analytics, automated provisioning, payment cards, negotiation benchmarks, or AI contract reading. Those are enterprise lanes and dilute the flat-price, afternoon-setup story.

## Price (intent)

Published flat price, no demo required. Pricing page is later; research suggests staying well under “a few hundred bucks a month,” with cheap competitors anchoring around £75–£200 / €95–€190 per month.
